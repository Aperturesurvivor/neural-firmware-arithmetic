## Recommended final human checks

- Read every claim, table, and citation once in the rendered PDF.
- Confirm that the public release does not include local model checkpoints or
  credentials.
- For the workshop PDF, ensure no author name, affiliation, acknowledgment,
  personal URL, or identifying repository link appears in the PDF or
  supplementary material.
- At upload time, inspect the platform-rendered PDF rather than assuming local
  compilation is identical.
- Stop before the final public `Submit Article` or OpenReview submission
  confirmation if any metadata or license field differs from this checklist.

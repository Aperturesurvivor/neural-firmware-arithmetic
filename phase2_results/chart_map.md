# Phase 2 chart contracts

These figures were generated by `scripts/analyze_phase2.py` from the frozen
confirmatory summaries. PNG and PDF forms contain the same plots.

## `accuracy_by_split`

- Purpose: compare sequence-level exact-match accuracy across all arithmetic
  evaluation splits.
- Comparison family: grouped bars.
- Series: frozen base, learned all-layer LoRA, latent firmware, and direct
  firmware.
- Uncertainty: sample standard deviation across three independently trained
  seeds for LoRA and latent firmware. The frozen base and structural direct
  control were each evaluated once on the fixed evaluation set.
- Non-color encoding: distinct hatch patterns; exact percentages are printed
  for bars of at least 4%.
- Intended reading: deterministic state plus a learned bridge extrapolates
  much more reliably than ordinary low-rank adaptation in this bounded task.

## `primary_ood_per_seed`

- Purpose: show the paired, preregistered primary-endpoint comparison without
  hiding seed variability.
- Comparison family: paired slope chart.
- Pairing unit: training seed (401, 503, 601).
- Criterion line: 99% latent-firmware exact match.
- Intended reading: the latent bridge reached 100% for every seed, while the
  learned control varied from 5.3% to 38.7%.

## `language_preservation`

- Purpose: show the preservation criterion that caused the overall
  preregistered result to fail.
- Comparison family: two-bar comparison.
- Values: mean token-exact preservation across three seeds; error bars are
  sample standard deviations.
- Criterion line: 99%.
- Intended reading: the latent mechanism preserved far more of the frozen
  model's behavior than LoRA, but 96.3% still missed the fixed 99% threshold.

## Sample sizes and visual policy

- Random arithmetic splits: 300 fixed examples per seed and method.
- Carry-chain split: 150 fixed examples per seed and method.
- Preservation: 100 fixed control prompts per seed and method.
- Axes begin at zero. No truncated axis, smoothing, or selective omission is
  used. Colors are supplemented by hatches, markers, labels, and direct
  pairing so the comparisons do not depend on color alone.
